## What zodiac compatibility actually compares

When you select two Sun signs in a compatibility calculator, the tool checks one thing: the angular distance between them on the 360-degree zodiac wheel. That angle falls into one of five classical aspects — conjunction (0°), sextile (60°), square (90°), trine (120°), or opposition (180°). Each aspect carries a traditional meaning in relationship astrology.

A **conjunction** means two people share the same sign. They understand each other's core motivations instinctively, but they also share the same blind spots.

A **trine** (four signs apart) connects signs of the same element. Fire with Fire, Water with Water. The connection feels effortless because both people process the world through a similar lens.

A **sextile** (two signs apart) pairs complementary elements — Fire with Air, or Earth with Water. There is natural support without sameness.

A **square** (three signs apart) creates tension between signs that share a modality (Cardinal, Fixed, or Mutable) but clash in element. These pairings push each other to grow. Many long marriages have strong square contacts.

An **opposition** (six signs apart) places two people on opposite poles of the same axis. They see what the other lacks. The polarity can feel magnetic or exhausting depending on the people.

## Elements and what they mean for love

Astrology groups the twelve signs into four elements. Each element describes how a person processes emotion and connection.

**Fire (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius)** — expressive, spontaneous, needs excitement. Fire signs show love through action and enthusiasm. They lose interest when a relationship becomes routine.

**Earth (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn)** — steady, sensory, needs reliability. Earth signs show love through presence and practical care. They pull away from partners who are unpredictable.

**Air (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius)** — verbal, social, needs intellectual stimulation. Air signs show love through conversation and shared ideas. They feel suffocated by partners who demand constant emotional processing.

**Water (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces)** — intuitive, absorptive, needs emotional safety. Water signs show love through empathy and depth. They withdraw from partners who dismiss feelings.

Same-element pairs (Fire–Fire, Earth–Earth) score highest on instinctive understanding. Cross-element pairs (Fire–Water, Earth–Air) score lowest on natural ease but highest on growth potential.

## What the score does not capture

A Sun sign compatibility score is a single data point. It does not account for:

- **Venus signs** — how each person gives and receives love. A Capricorn Sun with Venus in Pisces loves very differently from a Capricorn Sun with Venus in Aquarius.
- **Moon signs** — emotional needs and security patterns. The Moon drives the private self that emerges after the first few months.
- **Mars signs** — passion, desire, and conflict style.
- **House overlays** — where your planets fall in the other person's chart. A partner's Sun in your 7th house has a different weight than their Sun in your 12th.
- **Aspects between charts** — the full synastry grid. A Venus–Mars trine between two charts can override a difficult Sun sign square.

## When to use Sun sign compatibility

Sun sign compatibility works as a first filter. It tells you the broad dynamic — whether two people speak the same elemental language, whether tension or ease is the default. It answers the question people ask first: "Are we compatible?"

For anything deeper, use the synastry chart. That compares every planet in one chart against every planet in the other, with real astronomical positions from the ephemeris. The score reflects more than one axis.

## A score is not a verdict

No compatibility score, Sun sign or otherwise, can predict whether a relationship will work. Scores measure astrological alignment. Relationships depend on communication, timing, effort, and choice. Use the score as a conversation starter, not a decision maker.
