## Why astrologers look at transits during breakups

A natal chart is fixed. It shows the sky at the moment you were born and does not change. But the planets keep moving, and their current positions form temporary aspects to your birth chart. These are transits.

When astrologers analyze a breakup, they compare the transit chart at the time of the split to both people's natal charts and to the composite chart (the chart of the relationship itself). Certain transits appear so consistently during relationship endings that they form a recognizable pattern.

## Saturn transits: the structural test

Saturn is the planet of reality, limitation, and accountability. Its transits last months — Saturn moves slowly — and they force an honest assessment of whatever they touch.

**Saturn transiting the 7th house** puts the relationship itself under scrutiny. The 7th house governs committed partnerships, and Saturn moving through it asks: Is this relationship real? Is it built on something solid? Partnerships that lack genuine foundation often end during this transit. Those that are structurally sound may feel heavy but survive.

**Saturn conjunct natal Venus** strips romance of its illusions. The transit does not destroy love — it tests whether love exists beneath the infatuation, habit, or convenience. If the answer is no, the relationship feels unbearable. If the answer is yes, the couple emerges with a more grounded connection.

**Saturn square or opposite natal Moon** creates emotional austerity. The person feels isolated, unsupported, and emotionally hungry. If the partner cannot meet them during this period, resentment builds. Many breakups during Saturn-Moon transits are not sudden — they are the culmination of a long emotional drought.

Saturn breakups tend to be final. They feel like a verdict. People who split under Saturn transits rarely get back together because the ending came from clear-eyed assessment, not impulse.

## Pluto transits: the forced transformation

Pluto transits are slower than Saturn's and more extreme. Pluto does not test a relationship — it transforms it or destroys it.

**Pluto conjunct natal Venus** rewrites a person's entire approach to love. During this transit, people often leave relationships that no longer match who they are becoming. The old way of loving dies. What replaces it may not emerge for years.

**Pluto transiting the 7th house** brings power dynamics in the relationship to the surface. Control, manipulation, dependency, and unspoken resentment become impossible to ignore. Some couples use this transit to rebuild the relationship on more honest terms. Others cannot survive what surfaces.

**Pluto square or opposite natal Sun** is an identity crisis. The person who entered the relationship may not be the person who exists after the transit. When identity shifts that dramatically, relationships formed under the old identity often cannot hold.

Pluto breakups feel like death. People describe them as losing a part of themselves. The grief is proportional to the transformation.

## Uranus transits: the sudden exit

Uranus is the planet of disruption, freedom, and sudden change. Its transits correlate with breakups that seem to come out of nowhere — though often, the groundwork was laid long before.

**Uranus conjunct natal Venus** electrifies the love life. The person becomes intolerant of anything stale, predictable, or constraining. Affairs, sudden attractions to unexpected people, and abrupt departures from long relationships all correlate with this transit.

**Uranus transiting the 7th house** destabilizes partnerships. The person may attract unstable partners, or they may become the destabilizing force. Either way, relationships that survive Uranus in the 7th house look very different on the other side.

**Uranus square natal Moon** disrupts emotional patterns. The person's needs change rapidly, and they may not understand why they suddenly feel suffocated by a relationship that previously felt comfortable.

Uranus breakups are abrupt and often shocking to the partner who did not see them coming. The person initiating the split has usually been suppressing a need for freedom that the transit finally made undeniable.

## Eclipse activations

Eclipses occur when the Sun and Moon align near the North or South Node. When an eclipse falls on a sensitive point in the natal or composite chart — natal Venus, the 7th house cusp, the composite Sun — it can trigger relationship turning points.

Eclipses do not cause breakups directly. They accelerate what is already in motion. A relationship that has been declining may reach its endpoint during an eclipse season. A relationship that is growing may formalize during the same period.

The eclipse axis matters. Eclipses along the 1st-7th house axis (identity versus partnership) are the most directly relevant to relationship shifts. Eclipses along the 4th-10th house axis (home versus career) trigger breakups when the couple's domestic or professional paths diverge.

## Patterns across multiple breakups

If you have experienced several significant breakups, mapping the transits at each ending can reveal a personal pattern. Common findings:

- **Repeated Saturn contacts**: The person consistently chooses relationships that cannot survive realistic assessment. The issue is usually in the selection criteria — choosing based on chemistry rather than compatibility.
- **Repeated Uranus contacts**: The person suppresses their need for independence until it erupts. The pattern often points to a natal Uranus-Venus or Uranus-Moon aspect that creates a lifelong tension between closeness and freedom.
- **Repeated Pluto contacts**: The person is drawn to transformative, high-intensity relationships. The breakups are devastating but each one leaves the person fundamentally changed.

Recognizing the pattern does not prevent it, but it allows conscious engagement. If you know that every seven years Saturn hits your Venus and forces a reckoning, you can prepare for that reckoning instead of being blindsided.

## Ex-compatibility and what it shows

Running a compatibility chart with an ex serves a different purpose than running one with a current or potential partner. With an ex, you already know the outcome. The chart explains the why.

Common findings in ex-compatibility charts:

- Strong Pluto contacts explain why the relationship felt impossible to leave.
- Saturn squares explain why it felt heavy or restrictive.
- Missing Venus-Mars aspects explain why physical chemistry faded.
- Nodal contacts explain why the relationship felt fated — and why the ending felt like losing a chapter of your life rather than just a person.

The goal is understanding, not reconciliation. When you see the astrological mechanics behind a past relationship's ending, the experience becomes less personal and more structural. That shift in perspective is often the beginning of moving forward.
