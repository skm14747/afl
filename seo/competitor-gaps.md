## Scoring methodology

Rows are scored with a SERP-derived proxy since Ahrefs traffic/value data is unavailable:

```
score = est_monthly_volume × competitor_count ÷ (10 + difficulty²)
```

| Variable | Definition |
|---|---|
| `est_monthly_volume` | Global monthly search volume (cross-referenced KeySearch + Keywords Everywhere) |
| `competitor_count` | Real peer calculator/tool sites ranking page 1 (excludes UGC platforms, Wikipedia, generic portals) |
| `difficulty` | 1–10 subjective scale: 1 = only UGC/thin sites on page 1, 10 = multiple DR 70+ authority sites |

**Higher score = more opportunity.** High volume + many peers (validated intent) + low difficulty = best targets.
